# Paid lower bounds for q-conjugated exits

## 0. Model and two counting lemmas

**Model.** We use the width-uniform local model of [coded-active-query-bound](../coded-active-query-bound/README.md) and [encoded-exit-obstructions](../encoded-exit-obstructions/README.md).

- Fields have owner width. One word is used at every tensor width.
- Free operations:
  - scalar owner mixing, with coefficients applied once;
  - COPY, CLEAR and owned workspace;
  - tensor-lifted one-column monomial address maps, meaning signed or phased affine permutations (the owner normalizers among them).
- Let Γ be the group generated by the free monomials F and the paid Gaussian words, which means Q and its conjugates by monomials, plus the stage Gaussians. A *paid call* applies a fixed element of C[Γ] to one field. Gaussians of any rank are the costed case; a rank-r call costs r^(1−C) ≥ 1.
- Lemma 1 of encoded-exit-obstructions (tensor powers of distinct operators are independent across widths) identifies width-uniform programs with computations in C[Γ] ⊗ V, where V is spanned by the input fields. Central phases are group elements, distinct from scalar coefficients.

**Lemma A (output-coset counting).** Let F ⊂ Γ be the free monomial subgroup. Define Π: C[Γ] ⊗ V → C[F\Γ] ⊗ V by u ⊗ e ↦ [F·u] ⊗ e, and drop the base coset F. Then the number of paid calls is at least rank Π(targets).

*Proof.*
- Every field is a C[Γ] ⊗ V-element.
- A free f acts by left multiplication. Left multiplication preserves right cosets F·u, so Π(f·x) = Π(x).
- Mixing and copying commute with Π.
- Inputs lie in the base coset.
- A paid call creates one new field, so it adds at most one vector to the span of the Π-images of all fields. ∎

This is the dual, output-side form of encoded-exit-obstructions' Lemma 2. That lemma groups by x·Mon and takes value spans over the inputs. coded-active-query-bound proves the 112-port single-stage case with kernel cuts and allows arbitrary paid unary maps. Lemma A is stated only for paid calls in C[Γ].

An independent review computed that the value form gives 232 at the nominated C_P cut, where Lemma A gives 240. The conclusions drawn from that cut below use only 232.

**Lemma B (suffix projection).** Fix a set U of input coordinates. A paid call on a field with no U-component produces no U-component. Hence the U-projection of the final Π-span is spanned by the outputs of paid calls that acted on fields with a nonzero U-component. Free invertible scalar mixing of the outputs, such as a return tail or LEFT, does not change that span.

So the number of paid calls touching U-data, in-place calls included, is at least the rank of the U-projection of the targets.

## 1. Single-stage conjugated codes (GLOBAL120)

**Lemma C.** Let r_a ≠ r_a′ be centre functionals on the nine-bit chart. Then [r_a·w] − [r_a′·w] is not of the form 2ℓ(w) + c (mod 4).

*Proof.* Reduce mod 2. The left side becomes (r_a + r_a′)·w, which is nonzero. ∎

The sign of q_a = i^(±[r_a·w]) is immaterial, because −[r·w] = [r·w] − 2(r·w).

Suppose the owners' centre functionals are pairwise distinct, and the scalar code X is pointwise in the common chart. Then:
- every entry q_a⁻¹ X_ab q_b with a ≠ b lies in a non-base coset;
- for a fixed input b, different outputs a lie in different cosets.

Transport to owner b's physical frame applies one fixed monomial per input column, so it only relabels cosets column by column. Hence the non-base supports of different rows of q⁻¹Xq are disjoint, and

> **rank Π(q⁻¹Xq) = the number of rows with an off-diagonal nonzero.**

A one-sided response q⁻¹Y puts row a in the single coset [q_a].

### Data

The owners are the 112 port normalizers of `coded-native-caller` and the 8 mode normalizers of `native-rank8-helper-provider`. The 120 centre lines are distinct under all four conventions checked: row 0 of G⁻¹, G e0, row 0 of G, and G⁻¹ e0.

The code is J = [[C, −4W], [3Z, 13 I8]] (`10-BALANCED-J-NATIVE-v1`), with W = M/2, Z = M^T/2, C = I − WZ and M^T M = 56I. The verifier checks J J⁻¹ = I. J⁻¹ has 6,720 off-diagonal nonzeros, in 3,360 distinct classes.

### Exit

The exit barJ⁻¹ = q⁻¹J⁻¹q has rank **120**. The old 112-port code gives **112**, matching coded-active-query-bound. The elementary decoder uses 240 calls.

### Nominated cut (4)–(5)

With B = J and s = X0 + X1, the target is:
- Y0 ← barB⁻¹ Y0 + q⁻¹ P B⁻¹ s
- Y1 ← Y1 + q⁻¹ B P B⁻¹ s

The free rows X0_out and X1_out are dropped; this can only lower the bound.

The decode part of the Y0 rows sits on Y0 coordinates that are private to each row. The Y1 rows sit on the X coordinates in cosets [q_a]. So the matrix is block triangular:

> rank = 120 + #{nonzero rows of B P B⁻¹} = **240**, for P = diag(I112, 0) and for P = I.

In the variant where Y0 was already decoded before the cut, each coset [q_a] needs (P B⁻¹)_a and (B P B⁻¹)_a. That gives **232** for C_P and **240** for C_I.

The uncoded C_P contact has rank 112, which equals the retained port-query bank.

The chart's resource at this cut is the retained 112-port query bank plus the eight declared mode calls, 120 calls in all. In either variant, the cut needs at least **112 additional paid calls**.

### Retirements

Take k = 0..14. QUERY_SHEAR(·, k) reads X_k and adds q⁻¹X_k to Y_k. The verifier checks the following, against the controller and the neutral literal invoice:
- LEFT, the return tail and the signed swaps are invertible: ADDs of ±1 between distinct banks, swaps, and SCALE by −1.

  - for k = 0..6 there are none;

Assume the chart's matched type: Y_k carries a q-conjugated balanced code after contact k. Assume also that the decoder sees Y_k's post-contact value with no correlated saved copy of earlier data. Take U = Y_k's fields after contact k.

The U-projection of the targets is then the decode, of rank 120. By Lemma B, at least 120 paid calls must touch U.

- **Pairs 0..6** have no scheduled call on Y_k after contact k. Apply Lemma B once, with U the union of the seven banks' post-contact fields, each taken as a fresh decoder input. The decodes are block diagonal on private coordinates, so the U-projection has rank 7·120. Hence at least **840 calls must be added**, or rerouted from other banks' scheduled work. Applying the lemma jointly matters: a single call on a field that mixes several banks counts only once.
- **Pairs 7..14** do have a suffix IDLE, which supplies exactly 120 calls on Y_k. The next subsection treats when that IDLE can carry the decode.

### When can an IDLE carry the decode?

**Lemma D (absorption criterion).** By the balanced-J intertwining (10-BALANCED-J-NATIVE-v1: R_pW = WR_h and R_hZ = ZR_p), J_t commutes with R_t = qD_t. D_t and q act on different axes. Physically, q² = X is the central Pauli X. Then

D_t·barJ_t = D_t q⁻¹J_t q = q⁻² J_t R_t q = (X⁻¹J_tX)·D_t.

So a paid layer containing the code's own stage Gaussian D_t, or a q, turns the q-conjugated exit into an ordinary one, X⁻¹J_t⁻¹X.

A stage Gaussian D_u with u ≠ t acts only on V_u. The stage geometry lists the other axes as unchanged spectators, and stage-t normalizers act on E+V_t. So D_u commutes with those normalizers, with q and with J_t. Hence it commutes with barJ_t.

**Assumption (code chart).** After contact k, the retiring bank's code lives in the chart of the pair's last FULL stage, s = 2k+1, as the chart's nominated matched Gamma(J1) does at contact 0. This is an assumption about the uninstalled 120-wide ABI. What the verifier checks is only that the last FULL before contact k is stage 2k+1.

In the pinned controller, every IDLE(p, H) is disjoint from pair p's own FULL stages; this is checked for all 15. Under the assumption, then, the suffix IDLE of pair k (history {2k+3, …, 32}) applied as scheduled leaves barJ_s intact. IDLE followed by any free post-decoder still carries barJ_s.

**Lemma E (in-sector factoring).** Let S be the private axes of the IDLE's stages. Suppose every non-IDLE operation on fields that carry U-data commutes with D_H; the in-sector condition. Examples:
- owner mixing, stage-s normalizers, central or own-stage Gaussians, and q-queries all satisfy it;
- stage-u normalizers or codes for u ∈ H act on E+V_u and do not. These are exactly the "IDLE pre/post codes" that this lineage proposes as its lever, so Lemma E excludes that lever.

Let N be the order of D_H modulo the free monomials. N > 1, since D_H is not monomial. Then:
- every word factors as D_H^m·w, with w free of IDLE factors and m mod N;
- by Lemma 1 of encoded-exit-obstructions, different residues give independent components.

The target is D_H·barJ_s⁻¹ and has residue 1. Apply Lemma A to the program in which each IDLE call is replaced by a free grade shift; the group is Γ × Z_N with free part F × Z_N. The q_a⁻¹q_b cosets stay private, so at least **120 non-IDLE paid calls** must touch U.

### A Gaussian-rank budget, and the IDLE pre/post-code lever

This subsection works in a Clifford alphabet. The free monomials are affine permutations with Z₄ phases of degree at most 2, and paid calls are Clifford Gaussians. This is narrower than coded-active-query-bound's free group, which also admits non-Clifford phases and permutations. Every Clifford word U acts on Pauli labels (x|z) by a symplectic map S_U, and the Clifford monomials are exactly the Cliffords whose S_U preserves L_Z = {(0|z)}. Define

> **κ(U) = dim π_X(S_U⁻¹ L_Z),** the dimension of the x-projection of U's Lagrangian.

**Lemma H (rank budget).** κ is unchanged by free factors on either side. For any paid Gaussian γ, κ(γ·U) ≤ κ(U) + κ(γ). A rank-r Gaussian, a product of r single-axis rotations up to monomials, has κ = r.

Consequently, every word produced by a program has κ at most the total rank of the paid calls along its path. By Lemma 1 of encoded-exit-obstructions, each target word must be produced exactly by some path. So that path carries paid rank at least κ(target word).

*Proof.*
1. Left factors: S_f preserves L_Z, so Λ(fU) = Λ(U).
2. Right factors: a monomial symplectic map acts on x-parts by an invertible linear map, so κ(Uf) = κ(U).
3. Paid factors: Λ(γU) = S_U⁻¹ Λ(γ). It contains S_U⁻¹(Λ(γ) ∩ L_Z), whose x-projection lies inside π_X Λ(U), with codimension at most κ(γ). ∎

**Exit words.** Let m be a monomial with linear part G. For a signed monomial (no S-type phase on the centre axis), κ(Q⁻¹mQ) = 2 when G e0 ≠ e0, and 0 otherwise. The x-part of the Lagrangian is spanned by z₀·G⁻¹e0 + (z·Ge0)·e0. The verifier computes κ for every supported word of q⁻¹J⁻¹q from the actual normalizers, under both readings of the linear part. All 6,720 off-diagonal words have κ = 2, and every diagonal word has κ = 0.

**Corollary (IDLE pre/post codes cannot carry the decode).** Model the IDLE as one parallel layer that applies D_H, of rank |S| = 8|H|, once to each field. Arbitrary free codes may surround it: owner mixing, codes in any stage's chart, cross-stage monomials that re-aim axes, and any workspace.

- Every off-diagonal target word, D_H ⊗ (q⁻¹ m_ab q) on disjoint axis blocks, has κ = |S| + 2.
- Every path through the layer carries paid rank |S|.
- So no choice of pre/post codes realizes the decode.
- Any completion must add **non-IDLE paid rank at least 2 on every off-diagonal decode path**. A path that bypasses the IDLE needs |S| + 2.

The Corollary needs no sector restriction. It does rest on three conditions:
1. the lever is a single IDLE layer with free codes around it, which is its proposed form;
2. the alphabet is Clifford;
3. the code's stage is not among the IDLE's stages.

Condition 3 follows from the code-chart assumption. If the code lived in a stage u that the IDLE supplies, Lemma D would give κ = |S|, and that IDLE could carry the decode.

### Consequences

- **The IDLE pre/post-code lever is closed** by the Corollary, under its three conditions: a single IDLE layer, a Clifford alphabet, and a code stage outside the IDLE. The decode needs extra paid rank on every coupled path of every retiring bank. The elementary decoder (q, then the code inverse, then q⁻¹) supplies exactly 2, using 240 rank-1 calls per bank.
- **In-sector, under the code-chart assumption.** All 15 retirements need at least 120 non-IDLE calls each on Y_k after contact k. Lemma E applies jointly, with one grading per IDLE. None of these calls is scheduled for any k. So at least **1,800 paid calls must be added**, or rerouted from later contacts or LOCALs of other pairs while those calls still meet their own targets.
  - Each added call costs at least 1.
  - The C* = 0.00330007 screen admits at most 916 additional rank-1 jobs, with the paid profile Popt held fixed. The rank-1 tariff is exactly 1, and higher ranks cost more.
  - **In this sector, the optimistic allowance cannot fund the Gamma termination unless at least 884 existing calls are rerouted, or a changed native FULL or contact places a stage-(2k+1) or central layer after each retirement (Lemma D).**
- **Without the sector restriction.** Lemma B gives at least **840** additional or rerouted calls for pairs 0–6, which is 91.7% of the allowance.
  - For pairs 7–14, the Corollary still forbids IDLE-only decoding.
  - But a count of the extra calls is not proven there, because one extra call can serve many paths.

## 2. Three fresh stages (odd-E8 negative exit)

### Premises

These are pinned by [odd-e8-negative-exit-algebra](../odd-e8-negative-exit-algebra/PROOF.md) (PR5508) for the PR5464 source:

- **(S1) Fresh stages.** There are three stages s = 1, 2, 3 with disjoint private spaces V_s and a common line E = ⟨e0⟩. The binary part G_{s,x} of each owner normalizer L_{s,x} maps E+V_s to itself and fixes V_t for t ≠ s.
- **(S2) Distinct labels.** G_{s,x} e0 = q_x^(s) ∈ E+V_s. This is the owner's label embedded in stage s. For fixed s, the labels q_x^(s) are distinct over the v = 112³ owners.
- **(S3) Stage form.** E_s = L_s⁻¹ A L_s with the same scalar A in every stage. The negative post-decoder is J = Q⁻¹ E1⁻¹ E2 E3⁻¹ Q.
- **(S4) Coset criterion.** If Q⁻¹NQ is monomial, then G_N e0 = e0. This is the source's four-destination expansion; only this direction is used.

For a path p: j → d′ (through E3⁻¹) → c′ (through E2) → i′ (through E1⁻¹), with composite monomial M_p, put

v_p = G_{M_p}⁻¹ e0 = G_{3,j}⁻¹ G_{3,d′} G_{2,d′}⁻¹ G_{2,c′} G_{1,c′}⁻¹ G_{1,i′} e0.

By (S4), two paths from the same input share a coset only if their v_p agree. A base-coset path has v_p = e0.

### Lemma G (private witness)

For owners d ≠ j, the path p₀: j → d → d → d has v = G_{3,j}⁻¹ q_d^(3). No other path from input j, ending at any output, has the same v.

*Proof.*
1. Let y = G_{1,c′}⁻¹ q_{i′}^(1) = y_E e0 + y_1, with y_1 ∈ V1. By (S1), G2 and G3 fix y_1.
2. Let z = G_{2,d′}⁻¹ q_{c′}^(2) = z_E e0 + z_2, with z_2 ∈ V2. Then v_p = y_1 + y_E·(z_2 + z_E·G_{3,j}⁻¹ q_{d′}^(3)).
3. The value w = G_{3,j}⁻¹ q_d^(3) lies in E+V3, and its V3-part is nonzero. Otherwise w = e0, which would give q_d^(3) = q_j^(3) and so d = j.
4. Compare components:
   - the V1-part gives y_1 = 0;
   - the V3-part gives y_E = z_E = 1;
   - the V2-part gives z_2 = 0.
5. So y = e0, i.e. q_{i′}^(1) = q_{c′}^(1), hence i′ = c′.
6. And z = e0, i.e. q_{c′}^(2) = q_{d′}^(2), hence c′ = d′.
7. Finally G_{3,j}⁻¹ q_{d′}^(3) = w gives d′ = d. ∎

### Corollary

Suppose (A⁻¹)_dd, A_dd and (A⁻¹)_dj are all nonzero. Then row d of J has a private non-base coordinate with coefficient (A⁻¹)_dd · A_dd · (A⁻¹)_dj. All rows that have such a witness are linearly independent.

### Data

The verifier checks, from the pinned K-TEMPLATE, NORMALIZERS and RESULT:
- the cube involution satisfies K² = I, KP = −P and K_aa = 0, where P = MM^T/4 and P_aa = 1;
- (A⁻¹)_dd = 1;
- A_dd = 1541/104663 for every d. This comes from the C_odd sector projectors (eigenvalues 98 and −1078, with diag(P/14) = 1/14), and agrees with the source receipt;
- (A⁻¹)_{(a,b,c),(a′,b,c)} = (K+P)_{aa′}/2, using K_odd = (K1+K2+K3−K1K2K3)/2, C_odd = (P1P2+P1P3+P2P3−P1P2P3)/2, K_bb = 0 and P_bb = 1;
- every base row of K+P has 48 off-diagonal nonzeros.

So every owner has a witness, and **every post-decoder of the emitted negative bank needs at least v = 1,404,928 paid calls**. The elementary decoder uses 2v.

PR5508 itself selects the pair ((1,0,0),(0,0,0)) through a different path, j → j → j → i. Its coefficient agrees with ours only because A is symmetric and A_dd is constant.

### Budget

The three-call word F1, B2, F3 (PR5464, odd-e8-lowrank-e-native) emits one negative bank of v owners. Its screen divides by 3v; indeed 61440/(3v) = 5/343 exactly. So the floor is **1/3 per port per local call**.

The margin, recomputed here, is 0.04382074131724228 per port per local call. A post-emission paid decoder would therefore consume the whole conditional margin **7.6 times over**, even with every other cost at zero. The N3 word has no IDLE and no paid call after the emission.

**Remark (full120, conditional).** Suppose (S1)–(S4) and nonzero witness coefficients also hold for full120's three-call endpoint over V = 120³ owners. That is not re-verified here. Then the same floor of 1/3 meets full120's declared margin of 0.08928848513036617, a factor of 3.7.

## 3. Scope

- **Post-decoders only.** The decoders in this note see the emitted bank and independent workspace. Correlated histories are outside the theorem, for example a pre-return copy, which encoded-exit-obstructions prices at 8 H8 against 112 H1 in its setting. So are changed targets and contact types, and paid calls outside C[Γ].
- **Not tight.** The bounds are counts; the elementary decoders reach twice them.
- **No κ claim.** No new κ, supplier or selected-result change is claimed.
